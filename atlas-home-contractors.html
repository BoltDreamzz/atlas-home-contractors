About Atlas

Old-school service.
Modern standards.

Atlas Home Contractors is owned by Bobby, who customers describe as an old-school contractor with a great personality — the kind who walks the property, talks through what the house actually needs, and gives a clear breakdown of the quote.

The company serves homeowners in Staten Island and focuses on residential exterior projects: roofing, siding, windows and custom front entrance doors. Customers have brought Atlas everything from a straightforward new roof to siding, windows and a custom entrance door on a 1905 home.

Reviewers consistently use the same words — honest, fair, accommodating, prompt, reliable — and note that the company is licensed and insured.
